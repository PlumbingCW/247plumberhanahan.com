Water, sewage or gas problem right now? Do these first

A few minutes of the right action limits damage while you wait for a plumber.

Shut off the water

Find the main shutoff where the line enters the house, turn it clockwise until it stops, and if you cannot close it call Charleston Water System at (843) 727-6800. For a single fixture, use the small valve under the sink or behind the toilet.

Cut power near water

If water is near outlets, a panel or an appliance, switch off that breaker only if you can reach the panel while standing on dry ground, otherwise stay out and call 911 or the electric utility.

Stop using drains

When a drain is backing up, stop running sinks, showers and the washing machine. More water has nowhere to go and the backup grows.

Smell gas? Leave first

If you smell gas, leave the building, then call 911 and Dominion Energy from outside. We do not respond to gas leaks ahead of the utility.

Call the 24/7 line

Tell the dispatcher what you see, where the shutoff is and whether anyone is at risk. That lets the plumber arrive with the right parts.

What an emergency plumber in Hanahan actually handles

After-hours plumbing problems usually fall into a short list. A supply line lets go and water runs under a floor. A kitchen line backs up on a Sunday with company coming. A tank water heater starts to weep at the base and the pan fills. Each of these is cheaper to fix the sooner the water stops.

We separate true emergencies from problems that can wait for the next morning, and we say so plainly on the phone. If a slow drain can wait until a same-day appointment, you hear that. If water is spreading, we treat it as the priority it is.

Leaks and burst pipes

A running meter with every fixture off points to a leak somewhere in the lines. Leak detection finds the spot before anyone opens a wall, and burst pipe repair covers the fix once the water is off.

Drains and sewer backups

A single slow sink usually needs drain cleaning. When several fixtures gurgle at once, the problem is farther down, and sewer line cleaning or hydro jetting clears the main.

Water heaters

A leaking tank is rarely a repair after a certain age. We look at the data plate, the source of the leak and your hot water use before suggesting repair or replacement.

Why Hanahan homes call us for plumbing problems

Hanahan grew fast in the 1970s. The population went from 8,376 in 1970 to 13,224 in 1980, so a large share of homes are now between 45 and 55 years old. Drain and supply lines from that period are now reaching the end of their service life, so aging pipe is a common cause of plumbing problems in homes this age.

Water heaters are different, since a tank lasts roughly 8 to 12 years and has usually been replaced already, so read the date on the data plate.

The city also sits at about 16 feet above sea level, with tidal water behind several neighborhoods. After heavy rain, a wet yard or a damp slab is not always a plumbing leak. A meter test with every fixture off tells you quickly which one you have.

Charleston Water System supplies most of the area, and its water is moderately soft at about 3.4 grains per gallon. That means mineral scale is not the usual reason a faucet or water heater fails here. Age, corrosion and worn parts are.

Not every call is urgent. Plumbing installation, water heater installation, repiping and sewer camera inspections are scheduled work, and we plan them around your calendar. Questions before you call

Do you handle emergency plumbing at night and on weekends?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day, every day. Nights, weekends and holidays go through the same dispatch, and we book the earliest appointment available. For active water damage, tell the dispatcher so it is treated as urgent.

How do I know a plumber is licensed in South Carolina?

The SC Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation (LLR) licenses residential plumbing contractors through the Residential Builders Commission. LLR licenses the individual, and a Certificate of Authorization covers the company. Ask for the credentials and verify them with LLR before work begins.

Will I know the price before the work starts?

Yes. The plumber diagnoses the problem and gives you a clear price before work begins. If the plan changes after the first look, you hear about it and approve it before we continue.

Which ZIP codes do you serve?

Our address is in 29410, Hanahan. We dispatch across Hanahan and to nearby ZIP codes in Goose Creek, North Charleston, Ladson and Summerville, including 29445, 29405, 29456 and 29485. Call with your address and we will confirm.

Should I call you or the water utility for a leak?

Call the utility when the problem is on its side of the meter, such as a main break in the street. Call a plumber for anything inside your property line. Charleston Water System is reachable at (843) 727-6800. If you are unsure, call us and we will tell you which side it sits on.

Can you come the same day for non-emergency work?

Sometimes. We offer same-day appointments when available. We will give you the earliest slot on the call instead of a vague promise.

Do you record calls?

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.
