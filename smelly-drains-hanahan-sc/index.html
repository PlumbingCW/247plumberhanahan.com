Cleaning Drains That Smell in Hanahan, SC

A smelly drain usually has a simple cause: a dry trap, a film of buildup, or food left in a disposal. A rotten egg or sewage smell that does not go away is a different matter and needs a closer look.

Call (855) 575-2890

By the Hanahan 24/7 Plumbing team · Updated 2026-10-15

To clean a drain that smells, first work out which smell it is. A musty or sour odor from one sink points to biofilm or food debris. A sewage or rotten egg smell from a drain you rarely use points to a dry trap. A smell that comes and goes across the house points to a vent or line problem.

Each cause has its own fix, and the wrong fix wastes a weekend. This guide covers the usual suspects in order of likelihood, and it explains when the odor is a safety issue and not a nuisance.

The dry P-trap: the most common cause

Every drain has a U-shaped trap that holds a small plug of water. That water blocks sewer gas from rising into the house. If the fixture is not used, the water evaporates and the seal is gone.

Run water into the drain for half a minute and the odor often stops at once. Check floor drains, utility sinks, a rarely used tub, and the lower bath of a home with a guest room. If the smell returns within days, the trap may be cracked, siphoning, or poorly vented.

Biofilm in kitchen and bathroom drains

Soap, skin oils, hair, and food make a slimy film on the pipe wall. Bacteria feed on it and make the sour smell. It forms in the trap, the overflow channel, and under the stopper.

Pull the stopper and strainer and scrub them with a brush and dish soap. Remove the P-trap under the sink, empty it into a bucket, and wash it out. Clean the overflow opening in a bathroom sink with a bottle brush, since the smell often comes from there and not the main drain.

An enzyme cleaner can help maintain a clean drain afterward. It acts slowly and will not clear a heavy clog. Skip the strong chemical openers, which can burn skin and are risky in a blocked drain.

Cleaning a smelly garbage disposal

A disposal holds food waste in places a spoon cannot reach. Shut the power off at the switch and the breaker before you reach in.

  • Use tongs or a flashlight to check for food stuck in the chamber. Never put a hand in.
  • Scrub the underside of the rubber splash guard with a brush and dish soap. Food collects there.
  • Grind a few ice cubes with a little citrus peel to knock loose film, then flush with cold water.
  • Run cold water for 15 seconds after every use.
  • Keep grease, coffee grounds in bulk, and fibrous scraps out of it.
  • If the smell remains, clean the trap arm below the disposal

A disposal that leaks or hums should be repaired before you clean it, not after.

Vent problems and why the odor shows up everywhere

Vent pipes run up through the roof and let air into the drain system. If a vent is blocked by a nest, leaves, or a crushed section, traps can be pulled dry as water flows past. You may hear gurgling and smell sewer gas at several fixtures.

A plumber can check vent flow from the roof or with a camera. Fixing a vent often means clearing it, not replacing it.

When it is the sewer line

A broken, offset, or root-filled sewer line can leak gas under the house, a damp smell near the foundation, or wet spots in the yard. Slow drains, gurgling in the toilet when the washer runs, and repeated clogs all point there.

A camera inspection is how a plumber confirms it. Our sewer inspection visits put a camera in the line and show you what is wrong before any digging. If the line has a bellied or cracked section, sewer line repair is the next conversation.

When sewer gas is urgent

CDC's NIOSH describes hydrogen sulfide as a colorless gas with a strong odor of rotten eggs. Exposure can irritate the eyes and respiratory system, and at higher levels it can cause dizziness, headache, and worse. Harm depends on the dose and the time exposed.

A faint odor that fades after running water is a nuisance. A strong rotten egg smell, headaches, dizziness, or burning eyes in a closed room is not. Open doors, leave the room, and call for help from outside.

The natural gas used in homes has an added sulfur-like odor too, so a rotten egg smell could be gas. If you cannot tell, leave the building, call 911, and then call the gas utility, Dominion Energy, from outside.

When this becomes an emergency

A sewage smell with water rising in a drain, a smell across the whole house, or any symptoms like headache and burning eyes needs fast help. Ventilate, stay out of the affected rooms, and keep ignition sources away until you know it is not a gas leak. What not to do about a drain smell

Do not mask the smell with fragrance sprays. Do not pour bleach and an acid cleaner together. Do not cap or tape over a vent. And do not ignore a smell that returns after the trap is filled, since that means something else is wrong.

What a plumber checks

A plumber smells and listens first, then fills every trap and runs fixtures together to see which one gurgles. They check vent flow, look at the trap arm, and use a camera if the line is suspect. They also check the toilet wax seal, since a failed seal leaks sewer gas at the floor.

Cost depends on access to the vent or cleanout, whether a camera is needed, and what the cause turns out to be. A refilled trap costs nothing, while a vent repair or line work costs more. We give a clear price before work starts.

Hanahan local note: warm, humid summers and dry guest-bath traps

Hanahan summers are warm and humid, which dries trap water faster. Warmth also speeds up biofilm growth in a drain that is used.

That is why guest bathrooms, spare tubs, and garage floor drains are the first places to smell in late summer. In a home that was empty over a vacation, run each drain for 30 seconds on return. If the smell comes back within days, call.

For a sewer backup outside the house, the City Public Works page lists Berkeley County Sanitation for sewer issues and Charleston Water System for water. Confirm which utility serves your street. Hanahan homes from the 1970s growth are now roughly 45 to 55 years old, so older drain lines are worth a camera look if the smell recurs.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my guest bathroom smell like sewage?

The trap is probably dry. Run the sink, tub, and toilet for a few seconds each. If the smell comes back in days, the trap or vent needs a look.

Is sewer gas dangerous?

At low levels it is mostly a nuisance. NIOSH describes hydrogen sulfide as having a rotten egg odor, and higher exposure can cause symptoms. Leave if you feel unwell.

Why do my drains smell worse in Hanahan in the summer?

Warm, humid weather dries trap water in rarely used fixtures faster and speeds up biofilm. Run every drain regularly in summer.

How do I tell sewer gas from a natural gas leak?

Both can smell like sulfur. If you cannot be sure, leave the building, call 911, and call Dominion Energy from outside.

Can a smelly drain mean a problem with Hanahan's water?

Rarely. CWS water is moderately soft and chloraminated, so the water itself is not the usual cause. Look at traps, biofilm, and vents first. If tap water smells, call Charleston Water System.
