Older ranch houses and what to watch

A brick ranch from the late 1960s or 1970s may have had some plumbing updated, but we cannot know which parts. Slow drains in several fixtures at once can point to the main line instead of a single trap. That is where a camera run through sewer inspection earns its cost.

Roots, bellies and corrosion are general causes of drain trouble in older lines. If a line backs up more than once, ask about hydro jetting after the camera shows the pipe condition.

Otranto Station and attached homes

Listings describe Otranto Station as attached units with an HOA that covers water and sewer. If that is accurate for your unit, a problem on a shared line may be the association's job and not yours. We cannot confirm it for any unit, so read your documents first.

Inside your unit, a leaking toilet, faucet, supply line or water heater is still a plumbing call. Water heater repair and toilet repair are the common ones.

Frequently asked questions

Is Otranto inside the City of Hanahan?

Yes. Otranto addresses we tested come back as Hanahan city on the US Census geocoder, and the city's 2022 plan includes Otranto.

How old are Otranto homes?

It varies. We saw one listing from 1969 and others described as brick ranches. We do not have a neighborhood-wide figure, so check the date on your own house.

My Otranto drain keeps backing up. What should I do?

Stop using water at the affected fixtures, and call. If more than one fixture is slow, the main line is more likely than a single trap, and a camera can show it.

Does the Otranto Station HOA cover plumbing?

Listings say it covers water and sewer, but we have not confirmed it. Ask your association which lines it maintains.

Do you serve all of Otranto?

Call with your street and we will confirm we can reach it.
