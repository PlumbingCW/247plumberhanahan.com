Plumbing questions for a 1990s house

A house from around 1995 is old enough that its first water heater has outlived a typical 8 to 12 year life. Whether yours was replaced is something to check on the data plate. The first sign of trouble is often small. A faucet that drips, a toilet that runs, or a slow tub drain is a good moment to look closer.

If you see gray plastic supply pipe near the water heater or under a sink, ask a plumber to look at the fittings, since that is the polybutylene era. We cannot say whether any Eagle Landing house has it. The only way to know is to look.

For a repeat clog in more than one fixture, a camera look through sewer inspection shows the line itself. For a single slow sink, drain cleaning is usually enough.

If water is leaking in your Eagle Landing home

These steps limit damage while you wait for help.

  1. 1

    Close the main shutoff

    Turn it clockwise until it stops. If it will not move, do not force it.

  2. 2

    Keep power away from water

    Stay out of standing water near outlets, and use the breaker only if you can reach it dry.

  3. 3

    Stop the heater if it is the source

    Turn off the gas or the breaker so the tank does not run dry.

  4. 4

    Move belongings

    Lift rugs, boxes and electronics off the floor.

  5. 5

    Call and describe it

    Give the street, what you see, and whether the main is closed.

Frequently asked questions

Is Eagle Landing inside the City of Hanahan?

Yes. Eagle Landing Boulevard addresses test as inside Hanahan city limits on the US Census geocoder, and the city's 2022 plan treats Eagle Landing as part of Hanahan.

Do Eagle Landing homes have polybutylene pipe?

We do not know. Polybutylene was used from roughly 1978 to 1995, and many Eagle Landing homes are from the 1990s, so it is worth a look. Only an inspection of your own house can say.

How old is the typical Eagle Landing home?

One real estate data page lists a median build year of 1995. That is a market estimate and not a count of every home.

Should I replace a water heater that is still working?

Look at the date on the data plate. A tank past 10 or 12 years is worth planning for, since a leak at the base cannot be repaired. See water heater replacement for what that involves.

Does Eagle Landing have an HOA that handles plumbing?

One data page says there is no HOA. We have not confirmed it, so check your own documents.
