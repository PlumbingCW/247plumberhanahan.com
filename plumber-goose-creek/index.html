No hot water, a wet ceiling, or a bill that jumped: what the symptom usually means

A cold shower is a common reason for an emergency plumbing call in Goose Creek. If the tank is leaking from the bottom seam, it is finished and the answer is replacing the water heater. If the tank is dry on the outside and the water is merely lukewarm, an element or thermostat is often the culprit and a repair makes sense.

A stain spreading on a ceiling or a warm patch on a floor points the other way, toward a supply line. Those leaks run behind finished surfaces and keep going while you sleep, so leak detection with listening and pressure tools beats opening walls at random. A water bill that climbs with no change in habits is the third signal worth a call.

Emergency status depends on whether water is still escaping. If you can stop it at the main valve and nothing is spreading, we offer same-day appointments when available. If you cannot stop it, treat it as an emergency and call the 24/7 line.

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or active leak

These steps limit the damage while the 24/7 line gets a plumber moving toward Goose Creek.

Shut off the main valve

Turn the main shutoff at your house clockwise until it stops. If you cannot find or close it, call Charleston Water System at (843) 727-6800. If it is stuck, do not force it with a wrench.

Kill power to wet areas

Flip the breaker for any room with standing water, only if you can reach the panel while standing on dry ground, otherwise stay out and call 911 or the electric utility. Say so on the call.

Stop the heat source

If the leak is at the water heater, turn off the gas valve or the breaker so it does not run dry, only if you can do it without standing in water.

Clear the area

Lift rugs, electronics and cardboard boxes off the floor. Move pets and kids to a dry room.

Call the 24/7 line

Give us the address, what you see, and whether the main is closed. We start from there.

Drains that gurgle, back up or empty slowly

A single slow sink is usually a trap or a hair clog, and drain cleaning with a cable clears it in one visit. When the tub gurgles as the washing machine drains, or two fixtures back up together, the blockage is in the branch or main line. That is a different job and needs a longer cable or a camera.

Older lines are more likely to have belly sections where waste settles, and cast iron can corrode from the inside. Water in a floor drain after a heavy rain deserves a call, because the cause may be the line or may be the sewer connection, and the fix depends on which.

How a leak call runs from the first ring

Here is the order we follow when someone describes a leak and cannot find it.

  1. 1

    Triage by phone

    We ask where the water is, whether you can shut it off, and whether power or gas is nearby. That decides how fast we move.

  2. 2

    Meter test

    With every fixture off, we watch the leak indicator on the water meter. Movement means water is escaping somewhere.

  3. 3

    Narrow the zone

    We close isolation valves one at a time to learn whether the leak is before or after the fixture group.

  4. 4

    Pinpoint

    Acoustic listening and thermal imaging locate the spot so we cut the smallest opening possible.

  5. 5

    Price, then repair

    You hear a clear price before work starts. Then we repair, pressure test, and tell you what to dry out.

Frequently asked questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Goose Creek?

Yes for the main one. ZIP 29445 is Goose Creek's own code and covers the city's core. The closest neighbors are 29410 on the Hanahan border to the southwest and 29456 around Ladson to the west, and we serve both. Past those, ZIP centers 29406, 29486 and 29483 sit within about 10 miles of Goose Creek, so most calls from those areas are reachable too.

Is emergency service available in Goose Creek?

Yes. Emergency dispatch runs 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Call the line and describe what is happening, and we will tell you how to limit damage while a plumber heads your way.

Does Joint Base Charleston affect which utility serves my house?

It can. Homes on or near base property may be served differently from neighborhoods in the city proper, and we have not verified which provider covers which street. Ask your water utility, and check the bill or the office listed on it before you call a repair crew.

How do I know if a leak is inside the wall?

Look for a warm spot on a floor, a stain that grows, a musty smell, or a water meter that keeps moving with every fixture off. Any of these means water is escaping somewhere. A meter test and acoustic leak detection can find the spot without opening several walls.

Should I repair or replace an older water heater?

Tanks typically last roughly 8 to 12 years. If yours is in that range and leaks from the tank itself, replacement is the usual answer. If the tank is dry and the problem is an element or thermostat, a repair is often worth it.

How do I check that a plumber is licensed?

Verify any plumber with the SC Residential Builders Commission at the Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation (LLR). LLR licenses the individual, and a Certificate of Authorization is the company license. Ask for the name and check it before work starts.
