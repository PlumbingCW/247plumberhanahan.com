Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or active leak

Four moves you can make in the first few minutes while a plumber heads to Ladson.

Shut off the main valve

Turn the main shutoff at your house clockwise until it stops. If you cannot find or close it, call Charleston Water System at (843) 727-6800. In a rental, ask the landlord where it is, or use the fixture valve.

Kill power to wet areas

Cut breakers only if you can reach the panel while standing on dry ground, otherwise stay out and call 911 or the electric utility. Unplug anything on a wet floor from a dry spot.

Clear the area

Move belongings off the floor and keep children and pets out of the wet room.

Hidden leaks are common in rentals and apartments, and the corridor explains why

Busy roadside lots, rental houses and apartment buildings mean many Ladson properties have several people using the plumbing and fewer people watching the meter. That makes hidden leaks common. Leak detection starts with the meter and works outward, which finds a slab or wall leak without tearing up the whole floor.

Water heater failures come next, since a tank that quit in an apartment is the sort of call that cannot wait. If the unit is past its roughly 8 to 12 year range and leaking, water heater repair will not save it and replacement is the practical answer.

Frequently asked questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Ladson?

We cover 29456, the main Ladson code, and 29485, which also accepts Ladson as a place name and also serves Summerville. Along the I-26 corridor, ZIP centers 29420, 29486 and 29483 are within range as well, so most addresses near exit 203 or US-78 are reachable.

Is emergency service available in Ladson?

Yes. Emergency dispatch runs 24/7 and does not pause for nights, weekends or holidays. Call and tell us the street so we can plan the route.

Is my Ladson water from Charleston Water System?

Charleston Water System lists Ladson in its retail water area, but Ladson spans three counties and the utility depends on the street. Ask your water utility, or check the name on your bill.

Why do rentals along US-78 get so many leak calls?

When several tenants share a building, nobody tracks the meter. A leak can run for days. A monthly glance at the meter with fixtures off catches it early.

My water heater is in an apartment closet. Can you still replace it?

Usually yes, though closet installs need clearance for venting and relief piping. We check the space before quoting a price, and we coordinate with the property manager if needed.

How do I check your license?

Verify any plumber with the SC Residential Builders Commission at LLR. Check the individual, and ask about the Certificate of Authorization that covers the company.
